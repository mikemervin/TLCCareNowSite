SEO work on tlccarenow.com

Summary for ownership — June 2026

In one sentence: We set up the marketing website so Google can find us, list our pages, and send the right visitors (operators vs residents) over time—mostly through free search, not ads.

What we did

  • Gave Google a list of all key pages (home, Enterprise, Campus Care, contact, etc.)
  • Set each page with a clear title and description for search results
  • Registered the site in Google Search Console and submitted the sitemap
  • Verified we own the domain (DNS — do not remove that record)

Why it helps

  • Findability — People searching “TLC CareNow” or partner community names can reach our site
  • Operator sales — Enterprise page can show up for community decision-makers researching tools
  • Residents & families — Homepage and Book CareNow path for people looking to schedule care
  • No ad cost — Organic clicks are free once pages rank (ads are still optional for speed)
  • Reporting — Google will show what people searched and which pages they clicked

What to expect

  • 1–2 weeks — Google finishes reading the sitemap; brand-name searches improve first
  • 1–3 months — More queries and traffic in Search Console if we add content and partner links
  • Not instant — This builds over time; it is not a replacement for demos, referrals, or outreach

Next steps (recommended)

  • Request indexing in Search Console for the homepage and Enterprise page
  • Check Search Console monthly once data appears
  • Add a short page per partner community when ready
  • Ask partners to link to www.tlccarenow.com

Remember

www.tlccarenow.com = marketing site (what we optimized).
app.tlccarenow.com = where users log in and book (unchanged).
